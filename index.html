NIC Verification Privacy Policy & Agreement

ChatPro Legal Documentation • Version 2.4-KYC • Effective September 2026
Sri Lanka PDPA Compliant
Official Legal Notice & Binding Data Consent:

This document constitutes a binding statutory Privacy Policy and Consent Agreement between you (the "User" or "Data Subject") and ChatPro ("Platform", "We", "Us", or "Data Controller"). It governs the submission, automated OCR extraction, biometric verification, secure encrypted storage, and regulatory processing of your Sri Lankan National Identity Card ("NIC") and associated biometric liveness data pursuant to the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) No. 9 of 2022 of Sri Lanka, the Electronic Transactions Act No. 19 of 2006, and international data protection standards.

1. Identity of the Data Controller

The data controller responsible for the lawful processing, protection, and retention of your identity records is ChatPro Trust, Safety & Identity Verification Division. For inquiries, subject access requests, or regulatory disclosures regarding your NIC information, you may contact our designated Data Protection Officer (DPO) via the administrative portal or support channels at privacy@chatpro.app.

2. Categories of Personal & Sensitive Identity Data Collected

To establish verified trust, prevent fraudulent account creation, and guarantee the integrity of end-to-end messaging channels, ChatPro collects and processes the following categories of data:

  • Document Imagery: High-resolution optical photographic captures of the front (and/or reverse) of your official Sri Lankan National Identity Card (NIC).
  • Document Identifiers: Your official National Identity Card number in both legacy format (9 numeric digits + 'V' or 'X' suffix) and modern format (12 numeric digits).
  • Extracted Biographical Data: Optical Character Recognition (OCR) extracted details including your full legal name, date of birth, calculated birth year, and gender.
  • Biometric & Liveness Vectors: Real-time facial frame captures and depth/liveness telemetry vectors generated during interactive selfie verification to ascertain physical liveness and confirm 1:1 biometric correspondence against the portrait on your National Identity Card.
  • Technical Verification Telemetry: Timestamp, camera calibration parameters, IP address, user-agent data, and automated OCR confidence score indices.

3. Lawful Bases & Purposes of Processing

All collection and processing of your NIC and biometric data is carried out strictly under the following lawful statutory bases:

  • Explicit Informed Consent: In accordance with Section 6 and Section 12 of the Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022, you provide explicit, affirmative, and recorded digital consent by ticking the consent confirmation and executing the verification workflow.
  • Anti-Fraud & Impersonation Prevention: Safeguarding the messaging network against identity theft, malicious impersonation, bot registration, and unlawful abuse.
  • Platform Integrity & Regulatory Compliance: Satisfying mandatory Know-Your-Customer (KYC) identity assurance standards required for premium communication capabilities.

4. Biometric Data Processing & Purpose Limitation

Biometric face match vectors and liveness data are processed solely to verify that the person submitting the National Identity Card is the genuine, living legal owner of the card. We explicitly prohibit and pledge that:

  • Your biometric information is never used for commercial behavioral profiling, targeted advertising, or consumer surveillance.
  • Biometric vectors are not cross-referenced against external commercial or third-party marketing databases.
  • Raw liveness recordings are isolated from public chat streams and are not accessible by any other platform user.

5. Cryptographic Storage, Encryption & Access Controls

We implement state-of-the-art physical, electronic, and procedural security safeguards to ensure your identity records remain protected against unauthorized disclosure, interception, or destruction:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communication between your device, the verification SDK, and our cloud services is fortified with TLS 1.3 protocol encryption.
  • Encryption at Rest: All stored NIC image assets and database records are safeguarded with AES-256 military-grade encryption within segregated private storage buckets (nic-images).
  • Row-Level Security (RLS): Database-enforced Row-Level Security ensures that other chat contacts or third parties can never query or inspect your stored NIC photographs, numbers, or biometric telemetry.
  • Time-Limited Administrative Review: Access by authorized security administrators is restricted to compliance oversight and is executed exclusively via cryptographically signed URLs with a maximum lifespan of five (5) minutes.

6. Data Retention, Archival & Erasure Schedule

Your NIC verification records are retained solely for the period necessary to fulfill the verification and security objectives of your active account:

  • Your verified identity status is maintained as long as your ChatPro account remains active.
  • Permanent Purging upon Account Deletion: If you trigger account deletion via Settings > Danger Zone > Delete Account, all your stored NIC photographs, OCR data, and biometric metadata will be permanently, automatically, and irreversibly deleted from our storage buckets and database within thirty (30) days.

7. Sub-Processors & Identity Verification Partners

Identity authentication is supported by vetted verification service providers (such as Sumsub WebSDK and Supabase Cloud Infrastructure) who process identity documentation on behalf of ChatPro under executed Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) compliant with ISO/IEC 27001, SOC 2 Type II, and the Sri Lanka PDPA. No personal identity data is sold, monetized, or shared with advertisers.

8. Your Statutory Rights as a Data Subject

Under the Sri Lanka Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 and applicable global regulations, you are endowed with enforceable rights:

  • Right of Access: You may inspect your verified identity status and recorded NIC credentials within your Account Settings at any time.
  • Right to Rectification: If OCR parsing misreads your name or NIC number, you have the right to request re-scan or factual correction.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You may request complete erasure of your identity card files and biometric telemetry by deleting your account.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw your verification consent at any time; however, withdrawal will result in the suspension of verified ChatPro messaging services.
  • Right to File a Complaint: You possess the statutory right to lodge an official complaint with the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka if you believe your personal data has been handled contrary to law.

9. User Representation & Legal Warranty

By presenting a National Identity Card and initiating verification, you solemnly declare, warrant, and certify that:

  • The National Identity Card presented is your own authentic, legitimate, and un-expired official document issued by the Department of Registration of Persons of Sri Lanka.
  • You are not impersonating any individual, utilizing fraudulent or forged documents, or submitting stolen credentials.
  • You acknowledge that submitting forged or altered government identification documents constitutes a punishable criminal offense under the Penal Code of Sri Lanka and computer crime legislation.
10. Formal Declaration of Voluntary Consent

By clicking "Accept & Confirm Agreement", you acknowledge that you have carefully read, understood, and agreed to be legally bound by this NIC Verification Privacy Policy & Consent Agreement. You hereby grant explicit, informed, and unambiguous consent to ChatPro to process your National Identity Card and biometric liveness data as described herein.
